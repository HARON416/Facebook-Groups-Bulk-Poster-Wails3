A few things to know

What is a Facebook group auto poster?+

A Facebook group auto poster helps you share a prepared post across the groups you select. Postika combines a post editor, group selection, scheduling, and posting logs in one desktop app.

Is Postika a Chrome extension?+

Postika is a desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It uses an installed Chrome or Chromium browser for your Facebook sessions.

What do I need to run Postika?+

A supported Windows, macOS, or Linux computer, an installed Chrome or Chromium browser, and your Facebook account. Platform downloads are listed on the downloads page.

Can multiple Facebook accounts post at the same time?+

Yes. Multiple accounts can run their posting queues in parallel. Each account has a separate browser session, groups, drafts, and posting settings. Switching accounts doesn’t interrupt the other queues.

Does my computer need to stay on?+

Yes. Keep Postika open and your computer awake while a queue is scheduled or running. Queues and posting logs are not restored after quitting.

Does a submission mean my post is published?+

Some groups require approval. Posting logs distinguish published posts, pending approval, failed, skipped, and unconfirmed submissions. Check unconfirmed results on Facebook before retrying.
